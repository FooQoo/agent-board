A release that sends the results of the work to your iPhone: agents deliver results you can look at while they keep working, and the board notifies your phone and opens the result when you tap.

## Changes

- **Agents deliver results.** An agent records the first thing you can look at, such as a draft video or a UI capture, with `agboard result add`, and keeps working without waiting for a reply. A result has a short summary, and optionally an image and a link or storage location of the main artifact. Each result gets a version number, and an agent that retries the same delivery does not create a duplicate. A result is a notification that needs no reply; a choice you need to make is still asked as a question. `agboard result ls` lists a task's results, and `agboard skills results` tells agents how to use them.
- **Read results on the board.** The task details have a new **Results** tab, on the PC and on the phone. It lists the results newest first, each with an **In progress** or **Final** badge, its version, time, and summary, an image you can enlarge, and the link as text. A result can also be opened from an address that names the project, the task, and the version, and the board scrolls to it.
- **Get notifications on an iPhone.** Add the board to the iPhone's Home Screen, open it from the icon, and choose **Enable notifications** in the settings. No other notification app or account is needed, and the board keeps working if you do not allow them. Notifications are encrypted from your PC to the phone.
- **Choose what you are notified about.** **Phone notifications** in **Settings** switches four kinds one by one: results, questions that need your decision, card state changes (started, waiting for an answer, done), and history additions. Results and questions are on at first; the other two are off. Tap a notification to open the result or the card. Several changes to one card arrive as one notification, and the same result or question is never shown twice. Notifications are sent only while the remote connection is up; the board shows everything either way.
- **A periodic status notification.** Turn on **Periodic status notification** and choose 30 minutes, 1 hour, 2 hours, or 4 hours, and the board sends one notification per interval about the cards in Doing. It says how many records were written since the previous report, or that there is no new record and when the last one was. It reports the agent's state only when the board holds it, and never claims that work continues. It is off at first, and results and questions still arrive at once.
- **Your phone is remembered.** Entering the passcode registers the phone. After the 12-hour session ends, after you pause and resume the connection, or after the PC restarts, the phone opens the board again without the passcode. The connection dialog lists **Registered phones**, and **Remove** signs a phone out at once; remove a lost phone right away. The **Stop** button is now **Pause connection**, which keeps the token, the domain, and the registered phones.
- **Keep the same address.** In the connection dialog, enter a domain that your ngrok account owns (the **ngrok domain** field, or the `AGBOARD_NGROK_DOMAIN` environment variable), and the HTTPS address stays the same after every start, so bookmarks and Home Screen icons keep working. Without a domain, ngrok still chooses a random address.

## Fixes

None.

## Breaking changes

Two files saved by this version are refused by earlier versions of `agboard`. A task that has a result records it in its `task.json`, and a saved ngrok domain is written to `monitor-connection.json`. After you use v1.6.0 on a data area, do not go back to an older `agboard` on the same data. The other new files, such as the settings, the registered phones, and the push subscriptions, are not read by earlier versions.

## Required update steps

Update the command:

```sh
brew upgrade fooqoo/tap/agboard
```

Restart `agboard serve` after the upgrade. To get notifications on an iPhone, add the board to its Home Screen and enable them in the settings. `agboard update` is not needed: this release changes no hook command, so the plugin updates itself at the first agent session after the upgrade.
